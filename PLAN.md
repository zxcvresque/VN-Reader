# VN Reader direction

VN Reader focuses on reading a Telegram archive: readable posts, original quote context, navigation, bookmarks, and saved progress.

Interpretation belongs to the reader. The entity graph, alias correction, and standalone entity resolver have been retired, including their commands, generation scripts, and application dependencies.

The retained application has four views: Read, Threads, Bookmarks, and Progress. Telegram formatting entities and quote/reply metadata remain part of the archive format because they preserve the original messages.

Potential future reader improvements include passage annotations, font and theme controls, exact-position resume, quoted-source previews, personal collections, and reading-state backup. These are suggestions, not implemented features.
